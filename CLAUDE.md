# English Drill Book — notes for agents

Offline-first PWA for Russian speakers learning English from grammar workbooks (Vue 3 + TypeScript + Vite). Built specification-first with OpenSpec: read `docs/process.md`, then `docs/roadmap.md`.

- Work only inside an approved OpenSpec change (`openspec/changes/<name>/`). No code before the owner has approved its proposal, design and tasks.
- `docs/product/`, `docs/constitution.md`, `contracts/` and `openspec/specs/` are owner-controlled. Change them only through an approved change or when the owner asks.
- Requirement IDs (`CHK-005`) come from `docs/product/`; keep them in requirement titles and name them in tests (`@req CHK-005`).
- Review goes in rounds (constitution C-18, `docs/process.md` step 4). When the tasks of a change are done and committed, a reviewing agent with a fresh context, not the author, runs the review skill on the whole diff and adds `## Round <n>` to `openspec/changes/<name>/review.md`: `Reviewed: <hash>` as printed by `pnpm review:hash <name>` (online: it fetches `main` first, as CI does), and `Verdict: changes requested` or `Verdict: approved`, then its findings as `### F<n> <title>` and, if it likes, one `### Notes` for its own explanation, such as why it approves; it edits nothing else. The author then fixes or declines each finding and hands the change back to the same reviewing agent, resumed, for the next round (a new one only when it cannot be resumed, and its round says so under `### Notes`), which states every finding still open, before its own findings, as `- F<n>: fixed`, `- F<n>: not fixed — <what remains>` or `- F<n>: declined — <reason>`, exactly; lines indented under such a line belong to it (the reason continued, or how the fix was checked). Repeat until a round approves; the approving round adds `## Requirement coverage` and `## Outside the listed requirements` below all rounds. The check reads nothing else: any other section, line or heading fails it, and so does a heading or a round's own line inside free text, or an HTML comment (`<!--`) outside a fenced block (the full grammar is in the clarifications of design D2 of the change `review-rounds`, the rule on comments in design D2 of `review-follow-ups`). Any later change to a reviewed file, its mode included, changes the hash and needs another round.
- Never add text from a published textbook to this repository, in content, tests, fixtures, comments or screenshots (C-01). The owner's books live outside this repository; do not copy from them.
- Do not read anything outside this repository. Acceptance scenarios exist elsewhere and are not for you.
- Repository language is English; the app's interface is Russian only. Talk to the owner in Russian.
- OpenSpec telemetry stays off (`OPENSPEC_TELEMETRY=0`).
