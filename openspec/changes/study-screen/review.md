### F1 A reread of the unit that started before a check overwrites the result of that check

- Where: src/ui/study/unit.ts:194 (`refresh`), src/ui/study/unit.ts:264 (`keep`), src/ui/study/unit.ts:128 (`readUnit`)
- Problem: `refresh` (design D6, "another tab") reads the unit in two steps, the states of every exercise of the pack first and the attempts and drafts after, and applies what it read when the screen is quiet at the start and at the end. Nothing tells it that an action was kept in between. When the window gains focus, or another tab says it wrote, and the learner presses **Проверить** before the read ends, the states are read before the attempt is appended; `keep` then shows the new state, and `refresh`, finishing later, finds the screen quiet again (`busy` is back to false, `edits` did not change, a button has the focus) and puts the old state back. The check is in the log, the unit header counts the exercise as done, and the exercise itself says «В работе» with no verdict on any row and no score, until the page is reloaded. The same happens to a reveal, a change by hand and "mark as done". Reproduced on the production build on Chromium and Firefox: unit 1 opened, exercise 1.1 answered correctly, the focus moved off the fields, then in one task `window.dispatchEvent(new Event("focus"))` and a click on **Проверить** of 1.1. The header says «сделано 1 из 3», the block says «В работе», row 1 has no verdict; after a reload the block says «✓ Сделано верно 8 из 8». A learner gets there by coming back to the window with a click on **Проверить** (the focus event comes at the press, the click at the release) or with Study open in two tabs; the window is as long as the read, which is one read-write transaction for each exercise of the whole pack, so it grows with the book.
- What should happen: a read that started before an action was kept is thrown away, or made again. For example `keep` could raise the entry's `edits` (or a counter of its own) when it applies its result, so that the check `entry.edits !== before[index]` in `refresh` leaves that entry alone. `refresh` has no test at all: add an end-to-end test of Study being read again after work in another tab (design D6), and one for this order of events.

### F2 After a draft could not be saved, coming back to the window empties the fields

- Where: src/ui/study/unit.ts:198 (`quiet`), src/ui/study/unit.ts:205, src/ui/study/unit.ts:217 (`writeDraft`)
- Problem: when `putDraft` fails, `writeDraft` drops its timer and sets `draftFailed`; the answers now live only in the fields. `refresh` does not look at `draftFailed`: with no field focused it reads the stored draft, which is older or absent, and replaces `entry.answers` with it. Design D6 says a failed draft write "never blocks typing" and is retried on the next change; instead the next focus of the window erases what was typed, exactly when the app has just said it could not save it. Reproduced on Chromium and Firefox: `IDBObjectStore.prototype.put` made to throw for the store `drafts`, «is» and «am» typed in items 1 and 2 of exercise 1.1, a click on the unit's title (the header says «Не удалось сохранить черновик»), then a `focus` event on the window: both fields are empty.
- What should happen: answers that are not stored are never replaced by what is stored. `refresh` should skip an exercise whose last draft write failed (remember the failure for each entry, not only as one flag for the header), and the failed write should be tried again by `flush` and on the next reread instead of only on the next keystroke. Add a test that refuses the draft write and then gives the window the focus.

### F3 `finishUnit1` goes on to the next exercise before the check of the previous one has landed

- Where: e2e/study.ts:151 (`finishUnit1`), used by e2e/study-frame.spec.ts:100, e2e/study-frame.spec.ts:112, e2e/study-frame.spec.ts:129
- Problem: this is the failure the author saw once in "@req PROG-014 the counts follow work done in another tab" on Firefox; it is a race in the test, not in the app. I reproduced it: the test repeated 150 times on Firefox with 12 workers failed once, at e2e/study.ts:146 with `locator.check: Clicking the checkbox did not change its state`, on the first option of exercise 1.2. In the trace, the click on **Проверить** of 1.1 returns at 12364 ms and the `check()` of the option starts at 12374 ms and clicks at the point (934, 559) it measured then. The result of the check of 1.1 arrives after its write, a few milliseconds later, and adds a line of verdict under each of its eight rows, which moves exercise 1.2 down by about 200 px; when that happens between Playwright's measuring and its click, the click falls on something else and the option stays unchosen. The app side is ruled out by the code: the only thing that could take a chosen option back is `refresh`, and it does nothing while a draft timer is waiting (400 ms) or `edits` has changed, both true right after the choice. The start screen is not involved either: its counts are read in the order the writes were made (measured under 16 Firefox workers: the count appears at most 0.65 s after the focus event).
- What should happen: `finishUnit1` waits for each check to be shown before it touches the next exercise, for example `await expect(exerciseOf(page, "1.1")).toContainText("Сделано")` after each press of **Проверить**. The same pattern is in `studyStates` only with such a wait already, so nothing else needs it.

### F7 The text kept in the focused field is not stored, and the next reread replaces it

- Where: src/ui/study/unit.ts:262 – 276 (the branch of `reread` for the exercise being typed in), src/ui/study/unit.ts:348 (`flush`)
- Problem: when another tab checked the exercise whose field has the focus, `reread` takes the other tab's answers for every other field and keeps the text of the focused field. That text is no longer what the store holds: the other tab's draft and check now give that item another answer. Yet `edits` and `stored` stay equal, so the entry counts as saved, and no draft is written. Leaving the field writes nothing either: `flush` only writes when a timer waits or the last write failed. The next reread that does not see the field focused finds the entry settled and unchanged, and replaces the field's text with the stored one, silently; a reload shows the stored one too. Probed on Chromium on the production build at `3a7c749`: «ar» typed in item 3 of 1.1 and its draft stored; 1.1 checked correctly in a second tab (item 3 «are»); back in the first tab with a `focus` event: the exercise is done and item 3 still says «ar», as the new test expects. Then a click on the unit's title, the stored draft still holds «are» for item 3, and one more `focus` event puts «are» in the field; after a reload it is «are» as well. The learner saw their text kept, and then lost it without typing anything.
- What should happen: text the screen keeps over what was read is treated as an edit that is not stored yet. For example, in that branch, when the focused field's text differs from `fresh.answers[typing]`, count an edit (`entry.edits += 1`) and schedule the draft as `setAnswer` does, so that later rereads leave the exercise alone until the draft holds that text. Extend the new test in `e2e/study-reread.spec.ts`: after the other tab's check is shown, leave the field, give the window the focus again, and expect «ar» in item 3, before and after a reload.
